Website privacy / Приватность сайта

This page covers visits to iva-agent.com, not the data processed by a self-hosted Iva installation.

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, referring sites and devices. It uses first-party analytics cookies. The tracker does not load before consent. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. Query strings and fragments are removed from page and referrer URLs. Google may process data outside your country. Google privacy policy.

Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 12 months. Use Privacy settings to withdraw consent and stop future collection. Previously collected statistics are not deleted by this action. Hosting providers receive technical request data necessary to deliver the site.
