# Telegram delivery

Telegram delivers replies with the user's usual notification settings. A quiet
reply uses Telegram's silent notification: place `<!-- iva:silent -->` on the
first line of the final answer. The channel removes this service line before
delivery.

Choose quiet delivery for low-urgency information when the owner's rules call
for it. Keep the regular delivery for information that benefits from prompt
attention. Store a lasting delivery preference in the owner's rules after the
owner confirms it.
